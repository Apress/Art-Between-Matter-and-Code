# Albertini & Moioli

**Active:** 1990s–2000s  
**Based in:** Milan / Brera Academy  
**Relevant to:** Chapter 1 — §1.1.4.3

---

## Overview

Stefania Albertini and Gianpiero Moioli developed a shared artistic practice rooted in the Brera Academy tradition, extending it through new material and technological dimensions. Their work revolves around a foundational duality:

- **The machine** — rational, narrative, rooted in industrial logic
- **The bubble** — luminous, transparent, fragile, virtual, incorporeal

This duality expands from intimate sculptural objects to urban and architectural scale.

## Key Work

**Labirinto Verticale** (2000)  
Steel and iron, 530 (h) × 250 × 250 cm  
Dongchon Stadium, Ulsan Metropolitan City, South Korea

A monumental structure that transforms sculpture into an *inhabitable and symbolic space*, mediating between geometry, material presence, and collective narration.

## Practice

Their sculpture functions simultaneously as:
- A **device** — with mechanical, structural logic
- A **story** — a narrative that unfolds in space
- A **space to inhabit** — the viewer enters the work's logic

## Connection to Brera Legacy

As students of Brera's historic masters (Marchese, Cavaliere, in the lineage of Marini and Fontana), Albertini & Moioli extend the Academy's sculptural tradition while projecting it toward:
- Immateriality
- Virtual worlds
- Narrative environments

## Brera Academy Virtual Lab

In 2008, Albertini and Moioli co-founded the **Brera Academy Virtual Lab** — a pioneering digital sculpture and architecture laboratory. This became the institutional context for integrating Blender and digital tools into the Academy's curriculum.

From this shared foundation, Gianpiero Moioli continued developing these themes through Blender and digital environments, which form the technical and aesthetic backbone of *Art Between Matter and Code*.
