# Timeline: Sculpture from 1776 to Today
### Supplementary material for Chapter 1 — *Art Between Matter and Code*

---

## 18th Century — The Neoclassical Ideal

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1776 | Brera Academy of Fine Arts founded in Milan |
| 1787–93 | Antonio Canova: *Psyche Revived by Cupid's Kiss* (marble, Louvre) |
| 1800–05 | Canova: *Napoleon as Mars the Peacemaker* (plaster model; bronze cast 1811, Brera) |
| Late 1700s | Neoclassicism establishes the mental project — the *idea* — as autonomous from physical execution |

---

## 19th Century — The Lombard Tradition

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1820 | Birth of Vincenzo Vela (†1891) — dynamic forms, emotional depth |
| 1830s | Lombard sculptural tradition shifts focus from classical idealism to expressive materiality |
| 1858 | Birth of Medardo Rosso (†1928) — wax and plaster experiments, dissolution of form |
| 1859 | Birth of Leonardo Bistolfi (†1933) — monumental sculpture, plaster as autonomous artistic moment |
| Late 1800s | Gipsoteca Bistolfi established in Casale Monferrato |

---

## Early 20th Century — Abstraction and Spiritual Tension

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1868 | Birth of Adolfo Wildt (†1931) — spiritual abstraction |
| 1889 | Birth of Arturo Martini (†1947) — narrative mythology |
| 1901 | Birth of Marino Marini (†1980) — archetype and existential tension (*Cavalieri* series) |
| 1940s | Lucio Fontana develops the theoretical foundations of Spatialism in Argentina |

---

## Mid 20th Century — Spatialism and the Conceptual Turn

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1946 | Fontana publishes the *Manifiesto Blanco* (Buenos Aires) |
| 1947 | First *Manifesto dello Spazialismo* (Milan) |
| 1949 | Fontana: first *Buchi* (holes in canvas) |
| 1957–60 | Fontana: *Concetti Spaziali, Attese* — iconic slashed canvases |
| 1957 | Carlo Scarpa expands the Gipsoteca Canoviana in Possagno |
| 1960s–70s | Giancarlo Marchese and Alik Cavaliere (students of Marino Marini) expand sculpture toward narrative and environmental dimensions |

---

## Late 20th Century — Narration, Environment, Technology

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1982 | Studio Azzurro founded in Milan |
| 1985 | Studio Azzurro: *Stanze* — pioneering video environment |
| 1992 | Studio Azzurro: *Il giardino delle cose* |
| 1993 | Giancarlo Marchese: *Dialettica Formale* (glass and cast iron) |
| 1995 | Studio Azzurro: *Tavoli* — interactive responsive environment |
| 2000 | Albertini & Moioli: *Labirinto Verticale* — monumental steel, Ulsan, South Korea |
| 2004 | Gianpiero Moioli begins working with Blender |

---

## 21st Century — Digital, AI, Metaverse

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2008 | Brera Academy Virtual Lab founded by Albertini & Moioli |
| 2008 | Gianpiero Moioli: Blender Foundation Certified Trainer (BFCT) |
| 2010s | 3D scanning, photogrammetry, and digital fabrication enter sculptural practice |
| 2016 | Studio Azzurro: *Immagini sensibili*, Palazzo Reale, Milan |
| 2020s | AI image generation (Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, ComfyUI) as creative tool |
| 2022–25 | NFT, blockchain certification, and metaverse exhibition spaces |
| 2025 | Publication of *Art Between Matter and Code* (Apress/Springer) |

---

## The Continuous Thread

> From Canova's marble to Fontana's cut, from clay models to digital meshes —  
> sculpture is not a rupture but an *ontological expansion* of material, gesture, and authorship.
